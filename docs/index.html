Lite Launcher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Lite Launcher (az.lite.launcher) is developed by Ali Aghayev.

Lite Launcher does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.
  • There are no ads, analytics, crash reporters or third-party SDKs.
  • Home screen layout, folders, tasks, notes and the photos you add (copied and cropped into the app's private storage) are stored only on your device.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Permissions

  • Query installed apps (launcher intent) — to show and open your apps
  • REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES — to open the system uninstall dialog when you choose "Uninstall"

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page.
